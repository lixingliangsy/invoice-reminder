# Dunner — Integrations

## Export targets
- **Copy-ready emails** — paste into Gmail, Outlook, or your CRM.
- **Sequences + history** (Pro) — schedule and track reminders.

## API & webhooks
- **Team API** — generate brand-voiced reminders programmatically.
- Inbound payment webhooks (Waffo) update fulfillment; we do not touch the existing webhook handler.

## Honesty note on connectors
Direct accounting-app (QuickBooks/Xero) sync is not offered. Reminders are text; we don't claim a one-click send until it ships. Dunner drafts; you send and contract terms still govern.
