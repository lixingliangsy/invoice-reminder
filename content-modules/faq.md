# Dunner — FAQ

**Q: Does Dunner collect the payment for me?**
A: No. It drafts reminder emails; you send them and your contract governs collection.

**Q: Is my client data stored or trained on?**
A: Used only to draft your reminder; not trained on without consent.

**Q: Which tones are supported?**
A: Friendly, Firm, Final — escalate only with Final.

**Q: Is this legal or debt-collection advice?**
A: No. It's a writing aid; follow your contract and local laws.

**Q: Can I automate sequences?**
A: Yes, with Pro (sequences + history); Team adds brand voice + API.
